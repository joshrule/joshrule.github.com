title: "I own nothing"
---

Ownership is part of our basic mental furniture in modern western
culture. Consider my own story. When I left for college, my dad gave me
some tools, and now, I own those tools. A few years ago, I bought my
first car, and now I own that, too. The money sitting in my bank
account, I own. The things Amazon ships to my door, I own. The groceries
I buy, I own (until my kids eat them). This is how the modern western
world works. We own things and are lord and master over them---we can do
with them whatever we please.

The problem, however, is that ownership is an illusion. I am not
actually lord and master over the things I supposedly own. If I race my
car at 100mph down my residential street, I face legal consequences. If
my bank collapses, I'll be penniless. My groceries rot. My tools rust.
In reality, I actually have very little authority over the things I
supposedly own.

I don't even have authority over my own mind and body. I can't keep my
body from aging or force my heart to beat. I often struggle to remember
what I want to remember and to forget what I want to forget.

This lack of authority is simply part of being human. It's not like
there's someone out there hoarding all the authority, so that if I just
learn the right secret handshake, I'll gain the control I lack. We're
all subject to the same limits.

It's tempting to say that what it means to own something is to have
control *subject to* these sorts of unavoidable, common sense limits. To
own an apple is just to be lord and master over it subject to the
constraints of physics and biology. If I drop it, it will bruise, even
if I'd prefer it stay crisp. If I forget about it, it will rot, even if
I'd prefer it to stay fresh. That simply doesn't sound like I own the
apple, because I don't have the authority and control required to make
it do what I want it to do. Instead, I have at best a limited ability to
manage the apple, subject to a heap of outside constraints.

All told, I am convinced that I don't actually own anything, that I
don't actually have that kind of control over anything. In fact, none of
us do. [God owns everything]({% post_url 2026-01-15-God-owns-everything %}). We
own nothing.
